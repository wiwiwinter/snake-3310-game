# Privacy Policy: Snake 3310

**Effective Date:** November 20, 2026

Snake 3310 is an offline mobile game developed as an independent retro-inspired arcade application.

## Information We Collect
Snake 3310 does **not** collect, transmit, store externally, or share any personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or device data.

## Local On-Device Storage
The app stores only your local game progress and preferences (high scores, selected speed difficulty, wall mode, and sound/music toggles) privately on your device's internal app storage. This data never leaves your device and is removed automatically if you clear app data or uninstall the app.

## Third-Party Services
Snake 3310 contains no third-party advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or external network connections.

## Children's Privacy
Because Snake 3310 collects no personal data of any kind, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

## Contact
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on the project repository: https://github.com/wiwiwinter/snake-3310-game.
